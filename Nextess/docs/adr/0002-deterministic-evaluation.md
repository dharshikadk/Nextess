# ADR 0002 — Deterministic Authoritative Evaluation

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Authoritative correctness, rewards and progress are computed by deterministic versioned rules.

## Why

The product requires correctness, fairness and reproducibility. A probabilistic AI response cannot be the final authority for XP, coins or completion.

## AI role

AI may provide optional tutoring/explanation, but its output is advisory and cannot directly mutate authoritative learning state.
